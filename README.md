## 🇨🇳 中文

这是一个即时通讯技能，负责收发消息和管理群聊。发送与回复消息、搜索聊天记录、管理群成员、上传下载图片和文件、管理表情回复都在其中，还包括应用内 / 短信 / 电话三档加急，以及发送交互卡片（Interactive Card）。它能读取消息分享链接的快照，并做发送者姓名解析，还默认对消息做表情和更新时间等富化处理，方便后续判断。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 发送 / 回复消息，搜索聊天记录，读取分享链接快照
- 上传下载图片和文件，管理表情回复
- 应用内 / 短信 / 电话三档加急推送
- 发送交互卡片（Interactive Card）
- 搜索、创建群聊与话题群，管理群成员与置顶会话
- 默认富化消息（表情回复 / 更新时间）并解析发送者姓名

## 🇬🇧 English

A messaging skill that covers sending and replying to messages plus group-chat management. It searches chat history, manages group members, uploads and downloads images and files, handles emoji reactions, and can escalate with in-app, SMS, or phone urgency. It also sends interactive cards, resolves sender names, reads snapshots of shared message links, and enriches messages with reactions and update times by default. Group and topic chats can be searched, created, and managed, along with feed pins, tags, and chat files.

### ✨ What it does

- Send and reply to messages, search chat history, read share-link snapshots
- Upload and download images and files, manage emoji reactions
- Three levels of urgency: in-app, SMS, and phone call
- Send interactive cards (Interactive Card)
- Search, create, and manage group and topic chats, members, and feed pins
- Auto-enrich messages with reactions/update time and resolve sender names
